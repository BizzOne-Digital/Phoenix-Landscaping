# Phoenix Landscaping — Website Dashboard Guide

A plain-English guide for updating the website yourself. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## What this dashboard is

Your website reads all of its words and photos from one place. This dashboard is that place.

When you change something here and press **Save changes**, the public website updates straight away. There is no "publish later" step and nobody else has to approve it. Take a moment before saving, and always check the live site afterwards.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open your web browser.
2. Go to your website address and add `/admin` at the end.
   Example: `www.yourwebsite.com/admin`
3. Type your email address and your password.
4. Click **Sign in**.

You now see the dashboard.

**If it says the details are not correct:** check for typing mistakes, extra spaces, or caps lock being on. If it still fails, contact whoever set up your account.

**Staying signed in:** if you leave the dashboard open for a long time and then try to save, you may see a message saying your session has expired. Just sign in again and redo that change.

**Signing out:** the **Sign out** button sits at the bottom of the dark menu on the left. Always sign out on a shared or public computer.

---

## 2. Finding your way around

Everything is in the dark menu strip on the left side of the screen. It is grouped into four parts.

**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — the home screen of the admin area.

**Content** (the words on your site)
- **Services** — the services you offer.
- **Who we serve** — the types of clients you work with.
- **Four seasons** — the spring/summer/autumn/winter section.
- **Trust points** — the "why clients stay" cards.
- **Why choose us** — the "why Phoenix" reasons.
- **Testimonials** — real client reviews.

**Media** (the photos on your site)
- **Page photography** — the big banner photos at the top of each page.
- **Gallery** — your job photos.
- **Media library** — every photo you have ever uploaded, in one place.

**Business**
- **Site settings** — your phone number, email, business name and main headline text.

**On a phone or tablet:** the menu is hidden. Tap the **Menu** button at the top left to open it.

**"View public site" / "View site"** at the top right opens your real website in a new tab, so you can check your work.

---

## 3. The Dashboard home screen

This is the first screen you see after signing in. It gives you two things.

**A) The connection message at the top**

- Green icon, "Connected to MongoDB" — everything is fine. Your changes will show on the website.
- Orange icon, "MongoDB is not reachable" — the dashboard cannot talk to your content store. **Do not try to fix this yourself.** Contact your web team. Your website is still up and running; it is just showing its built-in backup content for the moment.

**B) The count boxes**

Each box shows how many items you currently have of that kind — nine services, four seasons, and so on. Click any box to jump straight to that section. If a box shows a dash (—) instead of a number, the connection message above will explain why.

---

## 4. How every content screen works

Services, Who we serve, Four seasons, Trust points, Why choose us, Testimonials and Gallery all work in exactly the same way. Learn it once and you can edit anything.

Each of these screens shows a **list** of the items currently on your website.

### The buttons at the top

- **Refresh** — reloads the list, in case someone else made a change.
- **New [item]** — adds a brand new item.
- **Search box** — type a few letters to narrow the list down. Clear the box to see everything again.

### Reading the list

Each row is one item on your website. Depending on the screen you will see a small photo, the title, and two useful columns:

- **Order** — a number that decides the position on the website. A lower number appears first. So an item with 1 appears before an item with 5.
- **Status** — a green **Live** badge means visitors can see it. An orange **Hidden** badge means it exists here but visitors cannot see it.

### To change something that already exists

1. Find the row you want.
2. Click **Edit** on the right of that row.
3. A panel slides in from the right side of the screen.
4. Change what you need.
5. Click **Save changes** at the bottom.
6. A small green message confirms it saved, and the panel closes.

### To add something new

1. Click the **New [item]** button at the top right.
2. The same panel slides in, but empty.
3. Fill in the boxes. Any box with a small red star next to its name must be filled in.
4. Click **Save changes**.

### To remove something

1. Click **Delete** on the right of that row.
2. A confirmation box appears asking if you are sure.
3. Click OK only if you are certain — **this cannot be undone**.

**Better than deleting:** if you only want to take something off the website for now, click **Edit** instead and untick the **Show on the website** box, then save. The item stays safely in your list and you can bring it back any time by ticking the box again.

### If something goes wrong when saving

- A red message at the top of the panel tells you what needs fixing. Correct it and save again.
- "Your session has expired" — sign in again and redo the change.
- "Could not reach the server" — your internet connection dropped. Nothing was saved. Check your connection and try again.
- **Cancel** closes the panel without saving anything.

## 5. The kinds of boxes you will fill in

| What you see | What to do with it |
|---|---|
| **Single-line box** | Type a short piece of text, such as a title or a name. |
| **Large box** | Type a longer paragraph. It grows as you type. |
| **A box asking for "one per line"** | Type each point on its own line and press Enter between them. Each line becomes a separate bullet point on the website. |
| **Order (number box)** | Type a whole number. Lower numbers appear first on the page. Leaving gaps (10, 20, 30) makes it easier to slot new items in later. |
| **Tick box, "Show on the website"** | Ticked means visitors see it. Unticked means it is hidden from visitors. |
| **Icon (drop-down list)** | Pick a small symbol from the list. The chosen symbol is previewed in the square to the left, so try a few until it suits. |
| **Photo box** | See section 6 below. |
| **Alt text** | A short sentence describing what is in the photo, for visitors who cannot see images and for Google. Example: "Trimmed hedge along a stone garden path." |
| **Slug** (Services only) | The short name used in the website address for that service. Use lowercase letters with hyphens instead of spaces, like `lawn-care`. Once your service is live, changing this can break links people have saved, so leave it alone unless you have a reason. |

## 6. Adding and changing photos

Wherever you see a photo box, it works the same way.

**To add a photo**
1. Click **Upload image**.
2. Your computer's file window opens. Choose the photo.
3. Wait a moment — a small loading bar appears.
4. The photo preview appears once it is done.
5. Fill in the **Alt text** box underneath.
6. Click **Save changes**.

**To swap a photo:** click **Replace** and choose the new one.

**To take a photo off an item:** click **Remove**, then save.

**Photo rules**
- Accepted types: JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF.
- Maximum size: 8 MB per photo. If yours is bigger, resize it first.
- Landscape (wide) photos usually look best in banners and cards.

**If you see an orange warning saying "legacy path"**: that photo was stored under an old system and no longer exists, so the website is showing a grey placeholder. Just upload a new photo over it.

## 7. Section-by-section notes

### Services
The service cards on the home page, the services page and the footer.
Fill in a title, a short description (the sentence on the card), a long description (the fuller explanation), a "What you get" list, a "Suitable for" list, an icon and a photo.

### Who we serve
The client-type cards on the home and services pages. Title, description, icon, photo.

### Four seasons
The four-season section. There are normally four of these already set up — usually you are just refreshing the wording or the photos rather than adding new ones.

### Trust points
The short "why clients stay" cards on the home page, repeated on the testimonials page. Title, description, icon. No photo needed.

### Why choose us
The "why Phoenix" grid on the home and about pages. Same fields as trust points.

### Testimonials
**Only add reviews a real client actually gave you.** Copy the words exactly as they wrote them.
- **Quote** and **Author** are required.
- Role, location and service are optional extras.
- While this list is empty, your website politely shows a "reviews coming soon" panel instead. That is normal, not a fault.

### Page photography
Two lists on one screen.

**Top list — Page photography.** These are the big banner photos at the top of each page: home hero, about page hero, services page hero, contact page hero, testimonials page hero, and the about feature photo. You **cannot** add or delete these — each one is a fixed spot on the site. You can only change the photo in it. Click **Edit**, upload a new photo, add alt text, save.

**Bottom list — Hero collage.** The four photos that sit next to the headline on your home page on larger screens. Here you can add, edit, reorder and remove tiles. Keep four showing as **Live** for the collage to look right.

### Gallery
Your job photos. Upload the photo, give it a title and alt text, set an order number, and tick **Show in the gallery**.

### Media library
Every photo you have ever uploaded, all in one place.
- The buttons across the top (**all**, **products**, **gallery**, **pages**, **misc**) filter the view to one folder.
- Each photo card shows its file name, folder, type, size and upload date.
- **Open** views the full-size photo in a new tab.
- **Upload an image** at the top lets you add photos in advance, before you attach them to anything. Choose which folder to file it under first.
- **Delete** permanently removes a photo. If any part of your website is still using that photo, it will show a grey placeholder instead. Only delete photos you are sure are unused. This button only appears for full admin accounts.

### Site settings
Your business information. It feeds the header, the footer, the contact page and what Google shows about you, so double-check it. It is arranged in four groups:

1. **Business** — business name, legal name, contact person, years of experience, website address.
2. **Contact** — phone, email, service area, city, region and country. Your click-to-call and click-to-email links are built from these, so a typo in the phone or email breaks them across the whole site.
3. **Headline copy** — the main tagline, the long and short descriptions, and the small trust badges under the hero headline (one per line).
4. **Job-scope notice** — the short notice on the services and contact pages that tells enquirers what kind of jobs you take on.

Change what you need, then click the save button at the bottom. If the fields are greyed out and you cannot type in them, your account is an editor rather than a full admin — ask an admin to make the change.

---

## 8. Account types

- **Admin** — can do everything, including changing site settings and deleting photos from the media library.
- **Editor** — can add and edit content, but not change site settings or delete from the media library.

Your name, email and account type are shown at the bottom of the left menu.

---

## 9. Everyday routine

**Before you change anything**
1. Open your live website in a second tab so you can compare.
2. Know exactly what you are changing and why.

**While you work**
1. Change one thing at a time.
2. Save.
3. Wait for the green confirmation message.

**After you change something**
1. Switch to your website tab and reload the page.
2. Check it looks right, on both computer and phone.
3. If it does not look right, go back and fix it — or untick **Show on the website** to hide it while you sort it out.

---

## 10. Quick answers

**I saved but the website looks the same.**
Reload the page in your browser. If it still looks the same, check that item's **Show on the website** box is ticked, and check the **Order** number — it may have moved further down the page.

**Something is in the wrong position on the page.**
Change its **Order** number. Lower numbers come first.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
It cannot be undone from here. Add it again as a new item, or contact your web team.

**Everything shows "Loading…" and never finishes, or I get a "could not reach the server" message.**
Check your internet connection first, then click **Refresh**. If it keeps happening, contact your web team.

**I forgot my password.**
There is no self-service reset. Contact your web team.

**Can I break the website from here?**
You cannot break how the site is built. You can, however, make it look wrong — by deleting content, hiding everything in a section, or entering a wrong phone number. Work slowly, and hide rather than delete when you are unsure.

---

## Golden rules

1. Hide, do not delete, when you are unsure.
2. Always add alt text to a photo.
3. Check the live site after every change.
4. Only add real testimonials.
5. Double-check the phone number and email in Site settings — they appear on every page.
