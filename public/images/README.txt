Put site images in this folder.

logo.png            The Phoenix Landscaping logo. Dropped in here, it is picked up
                    automatically by the header (see src/components/Logo.tsx).
                    Transparent PNG or SVG, roughly 400px wide, works best.

Any other photos     Add them here, then point src/lib/images.ts at them, e.g.
                    src: '/images/hero.jpg'

Suggested sizes:    hero / page headers ~2000px wide
                    service, season and audience images ~1200-1600px wide
